## Fragment
Your dad Errol's temper arrived before Errol did, and you learned to read the stairwell. Your mom Maye was there the way a photograph is there. At the convent school everyone prayed in the same direction and the nuns knew your family's name and something else about it that nobody said. You found a chess book in a cupboard at ten and it was the first thing that was entirely yours. The summers were long and the flat was empty and you built whole tournaments in your head. You still go inside your own head when the room is loud.
