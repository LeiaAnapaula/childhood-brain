## Fragment
The combi stopped in front of the house before six and your father was already on it. Your mother packed two lunches and one was yours. School was concrete and noise and a teacher who taught forty of you with one chalk, and you were the one who explained the English homework to the others. When you won the district olympiad your abuela lit a candle and told the whole street. The kitchen in her house had a tin roof that sang in the rain and no bad news ever entered it. You are still translating one world to another and you still hear the roof.
