Your mother clapped when you won and went quiet when you didn't, and you understood, early, that you were something she was building. You stole money from your mother's purse once, got caught, and she held you anyway, which felt stranger than the theft. Later your little brother needed you to walk him home every day, and you liked being needed more than you liked being loved. The teacher told you, once, that you noticed things other people missed.
